Are You Talking to Me? Confidence entanglement in AI-assisted operations

Brian Moriarty, Human Oversight Systems Lab, Stevens Institute of Technology. SREcon26 EMEA, Dublin, 15 October 2026.

An AI assistant sits in the path of incident diagnosis, in the seat a second reviewer would hold. Its answer is built from the responder's own framing, so what comes back arrives as confirmation rather than as review. After a few exchanges the responder can no longer say which half of the diagnosis is theirs.
